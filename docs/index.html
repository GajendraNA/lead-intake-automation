1. Submit a lead

Pick a sample or write your own. Use made-up details.

At least 15 characters.

Nothing you enter is saved or posted to Slack. The text is sent to an AI model (Groq) for scoring. A one-way hash of your IP address is kept for two days to limit each visitor to 3 runs per hour, with 50 runs per day for the whole demo.

2. What happens

The same steps run in the real workflow.

  1. Receive the leadWebhook accepts the request
  2. Validate and clean the inputValid email, enough detail
  3. Check the demo limitsProtects the free AI quota
  4. Enrich the companySub-workflow lookup (placeholder data in this demo)
  5. Score the lead with AIFit score, urgency, budget, one-line summary
  6. Route by tierHot and warm leads alert the team

How the workflow is built 
POST /webhook/demo-lead
  ├─ normalise fields, trim and cap lengths
  ├─ valid email and message?  ── no ──▶ 400
  ├─ per-visitor and daily limits? ─ over ─▶ 429
  ├─ enrich company (sub-workflow)
  ├─ score with Groq (fit 0-100, urgency, budget, summary)
  └─ tier: hot ≥ 70 · warm ≥ 40 · cold · needs review (no score)

The production workflow (WF1) also saves the lead to Postgres and
posts hot and warm leads to Slack. A separate error workflow (WF3)
reports failures.

The free server sleeps when idle, so the first run after a quiet period can take up to a minute. The page wakes it as soon as you open it.
